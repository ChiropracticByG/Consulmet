Before you begin
Consulmet Desk Health has been prepared for you by Chiropractic by G, following the in-person workstation and posture assessments carried out at your workplace.
A few things to know:
  • This app offers general, evidence-informed wellness guidance for office-based work — it is not a substitute for individual medical advice or treatment.
  • Move within a comfortable range. Mild, short-lived discomfort with new movement is normal; sharp or worsening pain is not.
  • There is no single "perfect" posture — changing position regularly matters more than holding any one position perfectly.
  • If you experience any of the red-flag symptoms listed in the app, stop and seek advice from a healthcare professional promptly.
  • Everything you complete in this app is saved only on your device — no account, no internet connection required, and nothing is shared with your employer.
By continuing, you acknowledge that you are using this app voluntarily, that all movement carries some level of risk, and that you accept responsibility for staying within your own comfortable limits.
